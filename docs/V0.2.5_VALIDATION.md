### Dual-deck controller material correction

The first conversion of `EDJ_DJ_Controller_01` appeared plain gray/white because
the source uses legacy Diffuse, Glossy, Glass, and Emission shader nodes rather
than Principled BSDF. The asset converter now reads those shader colors and uses
an explicit controller palette: a black/charcoal chassis and jog wheels, dark
screens, and blue, green, red, gray, and white control accents. The corrected
P3D passed re-import validation, HEMTT check/build/release, and was deployed to
`releases/@edj` with the matching signature.

## Human asset acceptance

Status: **PARTIAL — MEDIA-PLAYER SCREEN RECHECK PENDING**

On 2026-09-11 the user confirmed that all ten assets have the correct scale and
work correctly in Eden/runtime. The sole reported exception was the media
player display image crop. Its nonessential glass overlay used negative UVs and
was incorrectly assigned the main texture atlas, causing unrelated atlas areas
to wrap across the screen. The overlay has been omitted while the correctly
UV-mapped screen underneath remains intact. A local textured render confirms
the intended waveform display; the corrected PBO still requires one in-game
screen recheck.

In Eden, search `mod (edj)` and inspect all ten objects under **Event DJ**:

- **Audio - Line Arrays:** curved four-cabinet array, straight four-cabinet
  array, and curved eight-cabinet stack.
- **Audio - Speakers:** curved and straight three-cabinet horn arrays.
- **Audio - Subwoofers:** straight three-cabinet subwoofer array.
- **Audio - Mixing Consoles:** digital audio mixing console.
- **DJ Equipment - Players:** DJ media player.
- **DJ Equipment - Mixers:** dual-deck DJ controller.
- **DJ Equipment - Controllers:** performance keyboard.

Place each beside a standing character and on a correctly sized production table.
Verify scale, orientation, ground contact, materials and normals, collision,
stable shadows, 1/5/15/40 LOD transitions, Zeus presence, and repeated placement. Speaker props
must remain visual objects and must not create audio streams.

Do not mark this catalog accepted until the in-game review is manually confirmed.
Stage selection is the next asset-design decision.
